# LocalAI for teams

LocalAI is **MIT-licensed and self-hosted** — teams run it without a vendor, on their own hardware, under their own policies. That part never changes.

For teams who want help getting there, we offer paid engagements that sit on top of the open-source project:

## What teams ask for most

- **Guided deployment.** We help your team land LocalAI on the right hardware (laptop, on-prem GPU, Kubernetes cluster, Jetson) and connect it to your identity provider.
- **Custom add-ons.** Office, Sandbox, Tables and the admin add-on library are open. We'll build or extend an add-on for your workflow — a custom MCP tool, a chat app for your product, a sector-specific skill.
- **Model-gallery curation.** A curated, signed set of models that matches your hardware and policy — no browsing 500+ entries to find the right fit.
- **Priority support.** A direct line to the maintainers for upgrades, incident response and roadmap input.
- **Training and office hours.** Short, role-specific training for admins, developers and end users.

## Who uses LocalAI this way

- Teams who need data to stay on their own machines — legal, healthcare, defence, finance, research.
- Teams who want a single API in front of many model shapes — chat, images, speech, embeddings — without switching providers.
- Teams who want to put an agent next to their internal documents and tools, running on their infrastructure.

## Pricing

Fixed-scope engagements, billed per milestone, no seat tax on the open-source project.

- **Deployment sprint** — one week, fixed scope. Hardware check, install, identity integration, a working chat and one add-on.
- **Custom add-on** — scoped to the workflow.
- **Priority support** — monthly retainer, response SLA, release-candidate access.
- **Training** — half-day blocks per role.

We'll quote exact numbers after a short scoping call.
